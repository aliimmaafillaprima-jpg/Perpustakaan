01 · INTRODUCTION

The library,
closer through digital access.

A school library helps students find reading materials and learning resources. LENDORA was developed as the digital library website of SMK Negeri 5 Surakarta to make collection information and library services easier to access.

Students can browse the catalogue, search for books, register or sign in, submit loan requests, and check their loan status. Administrators or library staff manage collections and student records, process requests, record returns, verify fine payments, and prepare reports.

Project goals

  • Help students find book information and recorded availability.
  • Support online book loan requests.
  • Help staff manage books, categories, students, and transactions in one system.
  • Keep return, fine, and payment records organised.
LENDORA manages library collections and lending services; it is not a full e-book reading platform or an online bookstore.

02 · WEBSITE FEATURES

Services at a glance

Features depend on the user's role. The catalogue and general information are public, while some transactions and management tasks require an account.

Catalogue and searchBrowse books and search by title, author, or publisher.
Book detailsView book information, category, and recorded availability.
Student accountsRegister and sign in to access the dashboard and lending services.
Loan requestsStudents submit a request; staff review and approve or reject it.
Returns and finesStaff record returns, book condition, and applicable fines.
Payment evidenceStudents submit evidence for staff review and payment confirmation.
Dashboard and noticesSummaries and notifications help users follow updates.
Staff reportsStaff can view history, download reports, and print available receipts.
Payment note: the DANA QR used by the site is static. Follow the displayed payment instructions and confirm the amount; staff verify payment manually rather than the system confirming it automatically.

03 · DEVELOPMENT TOOLS AND MATERIALS

What went into the website

LENDORA was developed using computer equipment, software, library data, and visual assets.

Tools and software

  • Laptop or computer: to write code and run the application.
  • Visual Studio Code: code editor for the project.
  • PHP and Laravel: application logic and server-rendered pages.
  • MySQL: storage for accounts, books, categories, and loans.
  • Composer: PHP dependency management.
  • Node.js and Vite: front-end asset tooling.
  • Blade, Tailwind CSS, and a web browser: interface development and review.
  • InfinityFree hosting: publishing the website on the internet.

Project materials

  • Requirements and role-access plan for students and administrators.
  • Sample book, category, stock, and loan data.
  • School logo, book cover images, interface text, and service rules.
  • Database structure for users, books, categories, loans, and notifications.
Technology versions may change with the project configuration. Sample data is useful during development but should not replace official library records.

04 · HOW TO USE THE WEBSITE

Start with the catalogue

  1. Open the websiteVisit https://lendora.infinityfree.me/ in a browser on a phone, tablet, or computer.
  2. Browse the catalogueOpen the catalogue to see books. Use search and categories to find a collection.
  3. Check book detailsSelect a book to review its information and displayed availability.
  4. Register or sign inCreate a student account using Register, or sign in with an existing account.
  5. Submit a loan requestSelect a book and provide dates according to the stated rules. Staff must review the request.
  6. Check your statusReview the dashboard, loan history, and notifications for staff decisions.
  7. Return the bookReturn the book according to its due date. Staff record the return date and condition.
  8. Follow fine instructionsIf a fine applies, follow the available payment instructions, submit evidence if requested, and wait for staff verification.
Tip: use your own email and password, do not share them, and contact school staff if an account detail or transaction status needs correction.

05 · CONCLUSION

Learning and reading,
within easier reach.

LENDORA connects the library catalogue with student and staff services through a website. Searching, loan requests, status tracking, data management, return records, and reports are part of one system.

Future improvements may include broader testing, better accessibility, updated catalogue information, and a smoother user experience. Accurate records and staff verification remain important for reliable service.
